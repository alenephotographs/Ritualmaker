- Track every reported planting/update as part of the running Ritualmaker operational ledger.
- Separate owned seed inventory from planted status.
- Separate direct-sown seed from transplant status.
- Preserve exact founder corrections over assistant guesses.
- Do not over-identify seedlings from cotyledons or unclear images.
- For root-competitive zones, favor hardy transplants, saved seed trials, pollinator/meadow logic, or amended pockets.
- For high-value production crops, prioritize the more protected, accessible, amended, and waterable zones.
- Treat roadside beds as both production and visibility infrastructure.
- Treat saved seed trials as selection experiments.
