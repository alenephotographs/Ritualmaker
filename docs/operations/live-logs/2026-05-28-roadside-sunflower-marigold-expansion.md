## 5. Method notes

Founder used a deliberate trench method:

1. Dug shallow trenches.
2. Placed sunflower seeds about **1/4 inch deep**.
3. Sprinkled marigold seed on top of/over the sunflower trench areas.
4. Covered with just a little soil.
5. Lightly tamped.

Operational interpretation:

- Sunflowers are intentionally set deeper and more anchored than surface-broadcast seed.
- Marigolds are functioning as a companion/fill layer over and around sunflower trench bands.
- The light tamp matters on the slope: it may reduce bird loss and washout, but heavy rain could still redistribute marigold seed.
