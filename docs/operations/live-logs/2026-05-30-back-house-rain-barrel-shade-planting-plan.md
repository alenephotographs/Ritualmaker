## 3. Operating recommendation

Do not treat this as a high-value food-production bed.

Best first use is a **rain-garden / shade-tolerant non-food edge planting**, or a controlled herb/green trial only if the edible plants are not directly under roof runoff and are grown in clean soil away from splash/overflow concentration.

---

## 4. Vegetable suitability

| Crop type | Recommendation | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fruiting vegetables like tomatoes, peppers, melons, squash | Avoid | Too shaded; likely poor production and disease pressure |
| Root crops like carrots, beets, radishes | Avoid under runoff | Food-contact/splash concern; uneven moisture; low sun may underperform |
| Leafy greens | Possible only as a small controlled trial away from direct gutter runoff | They tolerate part shade, but leaves are splash-contact edible tissue, so roof runoff is a concern |
| Herbs | Selectively possible | Parsley/cilantro/chives/mint-type herbs tolerate part shade better than Mediterranean herbs; rosemary should stay in a sunnier, protected garden area |
| Brassicas | Possible as a trial, not ideal | Broccoli/Brussels need more sun and time; shade may produce weak growth; better in protected garden/brassica zone |

## 6. Practical layout idea

If a rain barrel goes here:

1. Put the barrel on blocks or a stable base.
2. Direct overflow into a small stone/leaf-mulch basin, not straight into bare soil.
3. Create a planting crescent **downhill but offset from direct overflow**, not directly under the gutter stream.
4. Use non-food shade/moisture plants closest to the overflow.
5. Put any edible herbs only on the cleaner/drier outer edge where roof splash does not hit them.

---

## 7. Best current answer

For vegetables, this is not a strong site. The only edible category worth testing would be **part-shade herbs or greens**, placed away from direct roof runoff.

Recommended vegetable/herb options if founder wants a small trial:

- parsley;
- cilantro;
- chives;
- lettuce/greens only if clean soil and no direct roof splash;
- maybe kale as a rugged trial, but not as a priority crop.

Recommended not to plant here:

- tomatoes;
- peppers;
- melons/cantaloupe/watermelon;
- squash/gourds;
- broccoli/Brussels as main crop;
- rosemary.

Rosemary should remain in the sunnier protected garden/herb area.
