## 3. Operating recommendation captured

Treat this as a **water-management / shade-edge planting site**, not a primary food bed.

Best uses:

- rain-barrel access and overflow control;
- erosion-softening planting downhill from the outlet;
- pollinator/shade-tolerant ornamental or herbaceous edge;
- limited leafy-green trial only if water is clean, soil is not contaminated, and drainage is controlled.

Avoid placing high-value fruiting vegetables here as the main plan.

## 5. Safer planting direction

Best current recommendation for this exact site:

1. Do not make it a main vegetable bed.
2. Use it for water-loving or shade-tolerant non-food/pollinator plants if available.
3. If using vegetables, keep it limited to a small leafy-green trial in clean added soil, not direct roof-splash soil.
4. Keep rosemary in the protected garden, not under the rain-barrel/drainage area.
5. Maintain the drainage pipe function; do not block it with soil, mulch, rocks, or roots.
